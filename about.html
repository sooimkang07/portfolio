About me

I'm a product designer who leads with the system before the screen. My process starts with problem framing — mapping constraints, stakeholder incentives, and behavioral patterns before a single pixel moves. I care most about the work that happens before the design: defining what's true, what's broken, and what a solution must hold together.

I'm drawn to problems with competing constraints: where the right answer requires tradeoffs, and where a naive solution optimizes one variable while quietly breaking three others. I work well in ambiguous problem spaces because I treat ambiguity as data.

I'm increasingly AI-native in how I work — using AI as a research, synthesis, and decision-support layer rather than a shortcut. Outside of design, I think about food systems, urban infrastructure, and how physical environments shape collective behavior. More often than you'd expect, those threads show up in the work.
