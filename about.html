I'm a Product Designer shaped by storytelling, systems, and figuring out what actually matters.

Good products rarely fail because of the interface. More often, they're solving the wrong problem.

AI has become an integral part of how I work, helping me move faster from observation to iteration while staying grounded in real user behavior.
