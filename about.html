About me

I'm a product designer with a passion for the space where human behavior meets digital systems. My work sits at the intersection of research, strategy, and craft — I care deeply about why people do what they do, and I design systems that meet them there.

I'm drawn to problems that are deceptively simple on the surface but reveal rich complexity on closer inspection. The kind where the "obvious" solution turns out to be the wrong one, and where the right one requires you to understand something true about people.

Outside of design, I think a lot about food culture, language, and how physical spaces shape social behavior. Those interests end up in my work more often than you'd expect.
