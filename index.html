Sooim Kang Product Designer

Building digital products around the people who use them, not the technology behind them. Focused on AI, communication, and the systems shaping how we interact online.

Work

Organizing voice chats around topics, not timelines Annotating the web without losing context Controlling what you see in the feed Designing grocery shopping for what's actually eaten Restoring shared viewing in the streaming era Determining when health signals require action Transforming real-world inspiration into reusable design systems

Organizing voice chats around topics, not timelines. A platform that restructures how conversations flow, prioritizing topic relevance over chronological order.

Annotating the web without losing context. A web annotation tool that preserves source context while enabling seamless knowledge capture and sharing.

Controlling what you see in the feed. Giving users granular control over their feed experience through intelligent list-based curation.

Designing grocery shopping for what's actually eaten. Smart bundling that predicts consumption patterns and reduces waste through personalized recommendations.

Restoring shared viewing in the streaming era. A platform that brings back the magic of synchronous viewing in an increasingly isolated streaming landscape.

Determining when health signals require action. A monitoring system that distinguishes noise from signal, alerting only when intervention is truly necessary.

Transforming real-world inspiration into reusable design systems. A methodology for capturing insights from everyday environments and systematizing them for product teams.
