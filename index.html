Product designer Hi, I’m Based in New York

I design and build digital products that make complex interactions feel intuitive. My work explores AI, communication, and the tools shaping how people create and connect.

Selected Work

yap Notate Instagram Lists neuk

yap is a shipped voice messaging app that uses AI to turn long recordings into topics friends can listen and reply to individually, making it easier to catch up without losing the natural flow of conversation.

Notate is a published Chrome extension that attaches notes to HTML elements on live webpages, keeping ideas connected to their sources and organized across the web so users can revisit their ideas in context.

Instagram Lists is a feature concept that lets users group followed accounts into custom lists and browse by social context, giving people control over whose content they see while recommendations adapt within each list.

neuk is a service concept for booking private screening rooms where friends can watch together outside the home, connecting room discovery, reservations, contactless entry, and playback controls in one mobile experience.
