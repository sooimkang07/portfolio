Forage · Wearable + AI · FigBuild 2026 · Paused

Forage is a ring and AI library that lets designers capture inspiration with a gesture (an image, a color, a typeface, a motion) the moment they see it.

Problem

For designers, the world is an asset library you can’t “Save As.”

Designers spot usable moments everywhere, then lose them before they become material. Inspiration hits mid-movement, too fast to open an app.

Solution

Capture with a gesture. Find it later by what it is.

A ring captures in the moment. AI sorts each capture into an image, a color, a typeface, or a motion, and a library keeps it findable by type.

Research

Designers are stuck between finding inspiration and actually using it.

In design communities, the same complaints came up: feeds that won’t let you keep your place, and saves you can’t explain later.

The missing sense

We track our steps, heart rate, and sleep. We still can’t track inspiration.

Core flows

Capture alone isn’t the story; reuse is.

The full loop: capture, interpretation, retrieval, reuse.

Ring

Everything needed to capture fits on one finger.

A micro camera, motion sensors, gesture detection, and a small haptic buzz when it’s saved.

Archive

The archive sorts captures by what they are, not when you took them.

Browse the library by type and recency, group captures into boards, and inspect the palette, type, or motion AI pulled out.

Iterations

Built in 72 hours at FigBuild: four designers, one working prototype.

Research, brand, hardware form, and the app were built side by side. I led the AI product architecture, the working prototype, and the demo animations.

Design decisions

“Capture anything” was too broad. Four asset types made it teachable and buildable.

Limiting captures to images, color, typography, and motion made the system coherent. Capture is also limited on purpose: no faces, no sensitive places, and every signal is visible.

Guardrails

Capture is limited on purpose.

Reflection

Paused. The goal it was built for: less cognitive load, inspiration kept, an archive that’s yours.

The ring isn’t the strongest part. Finding the break between noticing and reuse, and designing a pipeline across it, is.

Success would be measured by whether designers pull past captures into real work, not by how often they save. See it on Devpost ↗

Takeaway

We can’t control when inspiration strikes, but AI and wearables can make capturing it as intuitive as one of our five senses.
